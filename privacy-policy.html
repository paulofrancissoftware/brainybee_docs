2. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

⚠️ IMPORTANT: BrainyBee is designed for and directed at children ages 5-15 years old. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable Canadian privacy laws including PIPEDA.

2.1 Parental Consent & Age Verification

Before collecting any personal information from a child, we require verifiable parental consent through our in-app consent flow:

  • Age Gate: Users must confirm their age category when first using the App
  • Parental Verification: Parents or legal guardians must actively check a consent box confirming they are the parent/guardian
  • Terms Acceptance: Parents must review and accept our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy before a child can use the App
  • Re-consent: If we make material changes to this policy, we will require new parental consent

2.2 Information Collected from Children

We collect minimal information from children:

  • Game Data: Scores, math problem responses, time spent on activities, achievement progress (stored locally by default)
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version (for compatibility purposes only)
  • Optional Cloud Sync: If parents enable cloud sync via Google Sign-In, we store game progress in the cloud

2.3 Parental Rights

Parents and legal guardians have the following rights under COPPA:

  • Review: Request to review personal information collected from your child
  • Delete: Request deletion of your child's personal information
  • Refuse: Refuse to allow further collection or use of your child's information
  • Revoke: Revoke consent at any time

To exercise these rights: Contact us at paulofrancis.software@gmail.com with the subject line "COPPA Request - [Your Request Type]". We will respond within 10 business days.

2.4 Teacher/Classroom Features

When using consumer classroom features, student progress may be shared with assigned teachers within the App only. No student information is shared outside the App or with third parties for advertising or marketing. Consumer classroom features require appropriate parental consent and teacher verification.

2.5 Brainy Bee Schools Addendum

Brainy Bee Schools is a school-managed version of the App for school-authorized educational use. For school accounts, access is managed by the school or district, and the consumer in-app parental consent prompt may not be shown.

Where permitted by COPPA, a school or district may authorize collection, use, and disclosure of student personal information on behalf of parents only for the use and benefit of the school and only in the educational context. We remain responsible for our own COPPA obligations and provide notice of our collection, use, and disclosure practices to schools.

School-version data may include school roster information, school email or account identifiers, display names, class membership, assigned classes or rooms, math practice results, student progress, and device-level preferences needed to provide the service. We use this information only to provide, secure, support, and improve the school-requested educational service.

Students may be able to control device-level practice reminder notifications. These reminders are for educational practice only and are not used for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

We do not use school-version student information for targeted advertising, behavioral advertising, sale, marketing profiles, or unrelated commercial purposes. We do not re-disclose education records or student personal information except to service providers that help provide the App, as required by law, or as directed by the school or district.

Parents, guardians, and eligible students should contact their school or district to exercise rights related to education records. Schools and districts may contact us at paulofrancis.software@gmail.com for access, correction, export, deletion, or to prevent further collection or use of student information where applicable.

Schools and districts may also have obligations under FERPA, PPRA, state student privacy laws, and local policy. Brainy Bee Schools is designed to support role-based access controls so students cannot view school directory information and teachers/admins see only tools available to their role.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Personal Data

Personal data may include information that can be used to identify or contact you:

  • Name: Display name only (not full legal name)
  • Email Address: Parent's email for consent; optional for cloud sync
  • Profile Picture: Optional, from Google account if cloud sync is enabled

3.2 Usage Data

We collect anonymous usage data to improve the App:

  • App usage statistics and feature usage
  • Performance data and error logs
  • Game completion rates and learning progress

3.3 Local Storage

By default, all game data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to any server. Cloud sync is optional and requires explicit parental consent.

3.4 Offline Functionality

📱 Offline Mode: The App functions fully offline. No data is collected or transmitted while offline. When connectivity resumes and cloud sync is enabled, only game progress data is synchronized.

4. Information We Do NOT Collect

🛡️ Privacy by Design: BrainyBee is designed with children's safety as a priority. The following features are intentionally NOT included:

  • 🎤 Microphone: BrainyBee does NOT access or use the device's microphone
  • 📷 Camera: BrainyBee does NOT access or use the device's camera
  • 📍 Location: BrainyBee does NOT collect GPS or precise location data
  • 📇 Contacts: BrainyBee does NOT access the device's contact list
  • 📸 Screenshots: BrainyBee does NOT take screenshots or record screen activity
  • 💬 Chat/Messaging: There are NO chat rooms, messaging features, or ways for children to communicate with strangers
  • 🌐 Social Features: Children cannot post public content, share personal information with other users, or create public profiles
  • 🔔 Unsolicited Notifications: We do NOT send push notifications to children without parental consent

5. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • Track learning progress and provide educational feedback
  • Enable optional cloud backup and sync (with consent)
  • Facilitate classroom features (with appropriate consent)
  • Improve educational content and user experience
  • Respond to parent/guardian requests and inquiries
  • Comply with legal obligations

6. Information Sharing and Disclosure

🚫 WE DO NOT SELL, RENT, OR TRADE CHILDREN'S PERSONAL INFORMATION. EVER.

We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With Parents: Upon request, we provide parents access to their child's information
  • Service Providers: Firebase (Google) for cloud storage and authentication, subject to their privacy policies and COPPA-compliant configurations
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect safety
  • Teachers: Only within classroom features, with parental and teacher consent

We do NOT share information with:

  • Advertisers or marketing companies for profiling
  • Data brokers
  • Social media platforms (except for optional authentication)
  • Any third party for behavioral advertising purposes

7. Advertising

No Third-Party Advertising

BrainyBee does not display third-party advertisements in the app. For children's privacy protection, we use the following safeguards:

  • No Ad SDK: BrainyBee does not use an advertising SDK to serve ads
  • No Behavioral Advertising: We do NOT use interest-based or personalized advertising for children
  • No Data Collection for Ads: We do not collect or share personal information for advertising purposes

8. In-App Purchases

👨‍👩‍👧 Parental Control Required: All in-app purchases in BrainyBee require parental authentication.

  • Authentication Required: In-app purchases require device password/biometric authentication controlled by the device's app store (Google Play)
  • No Direct Child Purchases: Children cannot make purchases without parental approval
  • Clear Pricing: All prices are clearly displayed before purchase
  • No Manipulative Tactics: We do not use manipulative tactics, pressure, or dark patterns to encourage purchases
  • Refund Policy: Accidental purchases can be refunded through Google Play Store within their refund window

9. Third-Party Services

We integrate the following third-party services, all configured for COPPA compliance:

Firebase (Google)

  • Cloud database and authentication
  • Analytics (configured for children's apps - no advertising identifiers)
  • Crashlytics for error reporting (no personal data included)
  • Privacy Policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Google Sign-In

  • Optional authentication for cloud sync
  • Used only when explicitly enabled by parent
  • Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

10. Data Security

We employ reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Encryption at Rest: Cloud data is encrypted using Firebase's default encryption
  • Access Controls: Restricted access with authentication safeguards
  • Local-First: By default, data stays on your device
  • Minimal Data Collection: We only collect data necessary for app functionality
  • Regular Review: We regularly review our data practices for security

Limitation: No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use industry-standard practices, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Data Retention

  • Local Data: Retained on device until manually deleted by user or app is uninstalled
  • Cloud Data: Retained while account is active, plus 90 days after deletion request
  • Consent Records: Retained for 3 years to comply with COPPA verification requirements
  • Backup Data: Purged within 30 days of deletion request
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated and anonymized; retained for product improvement

Contact us to request data deletion at any time.

12. International Data Transfers

🌍 Cross-Border Data: BrainyBee is operated from Canada. If you use our App from outside Canada, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in Canada and the United States (where our service providers, including Google/Firebase, maintain servers).

By using BrainyBee, you consent to the transfer of your information to these countries, which may have different data protection laws than your country of residence.

We ensure that any international transfers comply with applicable laws and that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data.

14. Do Not Track Signals

Our App respects "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals. When we detect a DNT signal:

  • We do not use behavioral advertising
  • We limit data collection to essential app functionality only

Note: As a children's app, we already limit tracking by default regardless of DNT signals.
