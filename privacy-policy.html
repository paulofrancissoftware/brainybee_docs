2. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

⚠️ IMPORTANT: BrainyBee is designed for and directed at children under 13 years of age. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable Canadian privacy laws including PIPEDA.

2.1 Parental Consent

Before collecting any personal information from a child, we require verifiable parental consent through our in-app consent flow. Parents or legal guardians must review and accept our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy before a child can use the App.

2.2 Information Collected from Children

We collect minimal information from children:

  • Game Data: Scores, math problem responses, time spent on activities, achievement progress (stored locally by default)
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version (for compatibility purposes only)
  • Optional Cloud Sync: If parents enable cloud sync via Google Sign-In, we store game progress in the cloud

2.3 Parental Rights

Parents and legal guardians have the following rights under COPPA:

  • Review: Request to review personal information collected from your child
  • Delete: Request deletion of your child's personal information
  • Refuse: Refuse to allow further collection or use of your child's information
  • Revoke: Revoke consent at any time

To exercise these rights: Contact us at paulofrancis.software@gmail.com with the subject line "COPPA Request - [Your Request Type]". We will respond within 10 business days.

2.4 Teacher/Classroom Features

When using classroom features, student progress may be shared with assigned teachers within the App only. No student information is shared outside the App or with third parties. Both parental consent and teacher verification are required for classroom features.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Personal Data

Personal data may include information that can be used to identify or contact you:

  • Name (display name only)
  • Email Address (parent's email for consent; optional for cloud sync)
  • Profile Picture (optional, from Google account if cloud sync is enabled)

3.2 Usage Data

We collect anonymous usage data to improve the App:

  • App usage statistics and feature usage
  • Performance data and error logs
  • Game completion rates and learning progress

3.3 Local Storage

By default, all game data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to any server. Cloud sync is optional and requires explicit parental consent.

4. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • Track learning progress and provide educational feedback
  • Enable optional cloud backup and sync (with consent)
  • Facilitate classroom features (with appropriate consent)
  • Improve educational content and user experience
  • Respond to parent/guardian requests and inquiries
  • Comply with legal obligations

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

WE DO NOT SELL, RENT, OR TRADE CHILDREN'S PERSONAL INFORMATION.

We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With Parents: Upon request, we provide parents access to their child's information
  • Service Providers: Firebase (Google) for cloud storage and authentication, subject to their privacy policies and COPPA-compliant configurations
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect safety
  • Teachers: Only within classroom features, with parental and teacher consent

We do NOT share information with:

  • Advertisers or marketing companies for profiling
  • Data brokers
  • Social media platforms (except for optional authentication)

6. Advertising

Child-Safe Advertising: BrainyBee displays advertisements through Google AdMob. For children's privacy protection, we use the following safeguards:

  • Child-Directed Treatment: We configure AdMob to treat all ad requests as child-directed (COPPA-compliant)
  • Contextual Ads Only: Ads are contextual and NOT based on user behavior or tracking
  • No Behavioral Advertising: We do NOT use interest-based or personalized advertising for children
  • Age-Appropriate Content: Ad content is filtered to be appropriate for children
  • Premium Option: Users can subscribe to remove all advertisements

7. Third-Party Services

We integrate the following third-party services, configured for COPPA compliance:

Firebase (Google)

  • Cloud database and authentication
  • Analytics (configured for children's apps)
  • Crashlytics for error reporting
  • Privacy Policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Google AdMob

  • Contextual advertising (child-directed treatment enabled)
  • Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Google Sign-In

  • Optional authentication for cloud sync
  • Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

8. Data Security

We employ reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Encryption at Rest: Cloud data is encrypted using Firebase's default encryption
  • Access Controls: Restricted access with authentication safeguards
  • Local-First: By default, data stays on your device

Limitation: No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use industry-standard practices, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

  • Local Data: Retained on device until manually deleted by user
  • Cloud Data: Retained while account is active, plus 90 days after deletion request
  • Consent Records: Retained for 3 years to comply with COPPA
  • Backup Data: Purged within 30 days of deletion request

Contact us to request data deletion at any time.
